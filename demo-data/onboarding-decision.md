# Northstar Labs — Onboarding Decision

Date: 2026-06-12

The operations team agreed to replace the single three-hour onboarding session
with three shorter modules. New employees complete the security and account
setup module before their first day. During their first week, they attend a
product overview and a role-specific workflow session.

Each new employee receives an onboarding buddy for the first four weeks. The
buddy answers day-to-day questions, while the employee's manager remains
responsible for role expectations and the 30-day review.

The team will evaluate completion rate, time to first independent task, and
new-hire feedback after the first two cohorts.
