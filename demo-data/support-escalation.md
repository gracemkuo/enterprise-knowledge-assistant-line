# Northstar Labs — Support Escalation Guide

A customer case is critical when it blocks a production workflow for multiple
users, creates a credible data-loss risk, or involves suspected unauthorized
access.

For a critical case, the support owner must notify the incident lead within 15
minutes and create an incident channel. The engineering on-call acknowledges
the case within 20 minutes. Customer updates are sent every 30 minutes until a
workaround is available, then hourly until resolution.

Billing questions, single-user usability problems, and feature requests remain
in the standard support queue unless there is verified broad production impact.
